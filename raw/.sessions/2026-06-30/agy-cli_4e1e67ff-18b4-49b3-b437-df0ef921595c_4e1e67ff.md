Google's **Gemini** models are trained and served using Google's proprietary **Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)**, which are custom-designed AI accelerators purpose-built for large-scale machine learning workloads.

### Key Infrastructure Aspects
*   **Purpose-Built for Gemini:** Gemini is developed, trained, and served almost entirely on TPU infrastructure. This allows Google to optimize the hardware, networking, and software stack specifically for the requirements of its frontier models, including multimodality and agentic reasoning.
*   **System Architecture:** TPUs are deployed in massive clusters known as **TPU Pods**. These clusters utilize a high-speed **Inter-Chip Interconnect (ICI)** and **Optical Circuit Switching (OCS)**, which allow for dynamic, low-latency reconfiguration of the datacenter network, enabling thousands of chips to act as a unified, high-performance system.
*   **Hardware Evolution:**
    *   **Newer Generations:** Google recently announced its eighth generation of TPUs, comprising two specialized architectures: **TPU 8t** (optimized for training) and **TPU 8i** (optimized for inference and agentic workloads).

By using vertically integrated hardware—designing the chips alongside the models—Google aims to maximize "goodput" (actual compute cycles spent on learning) and improve energy efficiency, providing a strategic advantage in performance-per-watt and operational cost compared to standard general-purpose GPU clusters.
